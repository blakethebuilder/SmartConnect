Privacy Policy

SmartConnect is operated by Smart Integrate (Pty) Ltd, South Africa.

What we store: your business name, email and password (hashed); the WhatsApp numbers of contacts who opt in by replying to your number; message content needed to deliver missed-call replies and broadcasts; and call records (caller number, time, status) from your connected Yeastar PBX.

What we don't do: sell your data, share contact details with third parties, or send messages to anyone who hasn't opted in or hasn't called you. Anyone can reply STOP to opt out at any time, and we process that automatically.

Deletion: ask us to delete your workspace and we remove all associated data within 7 days. Email hello@smartintegrate.co.za.
